FAQ

Common questions

How does Hold Keepers connect to Stripe? ▼

Through official Stripe Connect OAuth. You authorize Hold Keepers in one click with restricted permissions. We only modify coupons, discounts, and subscription pauses when a subscriber explicitly accepts an offer — we never touch credit card data or banking details.

Can customers abuse this to get endless discounts? ▼

No. You have full control over guardrails — e.g. one retention offer per customer per 12 months, minimum 60-day subscription age required. Abuse is blocked at the rule level before an offer is ever shown.

Do I need to write backend code? ▼

Not at all. Hold Keepers provides a 1-line modal script and a hosted cancel-page URL. Just point your "Cancel Subscription" button at Hold Keepers — the webhook handles everything else automatically.

What if a customer still wants to cancel? ▼

They're passed to your standard Stripe cancellation flow — no friction, no dark patterns. You still receive their verified reason so you know exactly why they left.
